Installation typically takes 1-5 working days, depending on the project size. We'll complete the work as quickly as we reasonably can without compromising on quality.

9.1 What We Need From You

We pride ourselves on clean, efficient working practices. To help us deliver the best results, please ensure:

  • Access: Our installers can access the site during working hours (9:00 AM-5:00 PM weekdays) or at agreed alternative times
  • Parking: Suitable parking and clear access for our van with an unobstructed route from vehicle to site
  • Neighbours: Consider informing neighbours who share access or might be sensitive to minor disruption
  • Utilities: Inform us of any electrical cables or water pipes you know about – we'll need locations of the main water valve and electrical fuse box
  • Power supply: Access to a standard 240V socket (complying with IEE regulations)
  • Facilities: Access to toilet facilities and a sink/water supply
  • Clear space: Remove breakable or valuable items from the installation room and along the route to it
  • Preparation: Remove picture frames, lampshades, soft furnishings, and existing furniture from the area
  • Electronics: Disconnect and safely store TVs, audio equipment, and media devices to protect them from dust

If we need to clear a room or dismantle existing furniture for you, this will naturally extend the installation time. If you anticipate any challenges in preparing the space, please let us know in advance so we can plan accordingly.

9.2 Protecting Your Home

We take great care to protect your home during installation. We use dust sheets, quality extraction equipment on power tools, and always perform a thorough final clean.

Please note: fitted furniture installation may occasionally cause minor damage to existing paintwork. If you're planning to redecorate, we recommend painting before installation, but be prepared for some touch-up work afterward. You'll also need to reconnect any TV or media cables after we finish and any paint has dried.

11. Fitting Tolerances & Finishing

When installing bespoke furniture, we carefully fill gaps between cabinetry and walls, floors, or ceilings using specialist fillers cut to suit your space and wall contours.

We work to tolerances of 1-7mm using high-quality, shrink-resistant white filling materials that leave no residue. When dry, these materials are paint-friendly if you wish to decorate, though decorating isn't our responsibility.

For projects involving multiple appliances, you might want to consider arranging a small skip for disposal. We're happy to remove appliance packaging if that's more convenient, but we'll need to pass on the actual disposal costs.
