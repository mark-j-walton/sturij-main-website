2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information that's necessary to provide our bespoke furniture services to you. This includes:

2.1 Contact & Identity Information

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Home address (where installation will take place)

2.2 Project Information

  • Your furniture requirements and specifications
  • Measurements and property details relevant to installation
  • Photos of your space (with your permission)
  • Design preferences and notes from consultations

2.3 Financial Information

  • Payment information (processed securely through Stripe – we don't store full card details)
  • Bank transfer details (if you choose this payment method)

  • Finance application information (if using Ideal4Finance)

2.4 Communication Records

  • Emails, messages, and notes from phone conversations
  • Project documentation, quotes, and contracts
  • Feedback, reviews, and satisfaction certificates

2.5 Website & Customer Portal Information

  • Login credentials and preferences for your personal project portal

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for legitimate purposes related to providing our services. Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

3.1 Contract Performance

We process your data to fulfill our contractual obligations to you:

  • Creating design proposals and quotations
  • Manufacturing and installing your bespoke furniture
  • Processing payments and managing invoices
  • Scheduling installations and coordinating with suppliers
  • Providing after-sales support and warranty services

3.2 Legal Obligations

We're required by law to process certain information:

  • Keeping financial records for tax and accounting purposes (7 years as required by HMRC)
  • Maintaining insurance records
  • Responding to legal requests or regulatory inquiries

3.3 Legitimate Business Interests

We process data where we have a legitimate business interest that doesn't override your rights:

  • Improving our services and customer experience
  • Protecting our business from fraud or legal claims
  • Analyzing business performance and trends
  • Honoring our lifetime furniture support commitment

3.4 Consent

For certain activities, we rely on your consent:

  • Marketing communications to existing customers (implied consent when you place an order)
  • Using photographs of completed installations in our marketing materials

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We only share information when necessary to deliver our services:

4.1 Essential Service Providers

  • Material suppliers: Name, address, and delivery requirements for order fulfillment (only when you've placed an order)
  • Payment processors: Stripe (for card payments) – they handle payment data securely under their own privacy policy
  • Finance provider: Ideal4Finance (only if you choose the finance option) – they conduct their own credit checks

4.2 Other Trade Specialists (With Your Knowledge)

If your project requires electricians, plumbers, or other specialists, we may share relevant project details with them (with your awareness and consent).

4.3 Legal & Regulatory Bodies

We may share information if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority (e.g., HMRC, police, ICO).

4.4 Insurance Provider

Zurich Insurance Company Limited (for claims management and policy administration).

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Enquiries that don't proceed: Deleted immediately upon your confirmation that you're declining our proposal
  • Customer orders: Retained indefinitely to honor our lifetime furniture support commitment and for warranty purposes
  • Financial records: 7 years from end of financial year (HMRC requirement)
  • Contracts and legal documents: 6 years after project completion (limitation period for legal claims)
  • Marketing communications: Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the following rights:

7.1 Right of Access

You can request a copy of all personal information we hold about you. We'll provide this free of charge within one month.

7.2 Right to Correction

If any information we hold is incorrect or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it immediately.

7.3 Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten')

You can request that we delete your personal information. We'll comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain it (e.g., financial records for HMRC).

7.4 Right to Restrict Processing

You can ask us to limit how we use your information in certain situations (e.g., if you contest its accuracy).

7.5 Right to Data Portability

You can request a copy of your information in a commonly used electronic format to transfer to another service provider.

7.6 Right to Object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. We'll stop processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds.

7.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making

You have rights regarding automated decision-making and profiling. We don't currently use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact Mark Walton at contact@sturij.com. We'll respond within one month.

8. How We Keep Your Information Secure

We take data security seriously and use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Secure cloud storage using Base44 database security policies
  • Secure payment processing through PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe)
  • Password-protected systems and encrypted data storage
  • Limited access to personal data (only those who need it for their role)
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Secure disposal of paper records when no longer needed

While we implement robust security measures, please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you become aware of any security breach, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your personal data is stored and processed in the United Kingdom. We do not routinely transfer personal data outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). If we ever need to do so, we'll ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information.
