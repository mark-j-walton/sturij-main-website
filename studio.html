Sturij · Pairing Studio

Start with two materials — the rest is yours to explore.

We've started you off with your wall colour and your board (door & panel) finish. Together these set the general scheme, so you can feel the space straight away.

Add any of the other materials to build a full interior scheme — an unrestricted playground to test as many variations as your project needs.

Want a hand? Use it alongside your own interior designer, or try our suggestions — drawn from Egger's own decor pairings and Farrow & Ball's colour schemes, so every combination is one the makers themselves would stand behind. Like a suggestion? Keep it. Don't? Swap just the parts that miss.

We've drawn on popular makers of paint, wallpaper and flooring, plus quality suppliers of the other pieces of a room — from switch finishes to worktops. More ranges are added over time, so check back on any home-improvement adventure.

Sturij designs, manufactures and installs bespoke furniture — or works supply-only with your own trusted trades. No-one's selling to you here; it's simply a lovely place to create.
