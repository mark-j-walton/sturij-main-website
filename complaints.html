Complaints Policy & Procedure

Storage Innovation Limited (Trading as Sturij)

⬇ Download PDF

We're committed to providing excellent service and the highest quality bespoke furniture. However, we recognize that occasionally things may not go as planned or you may have concerns about our service.

We value your feedback – both positive and negative – as it helps us improve. This policy sets out how we handle complaints fairly, promptly, and professionally.

1. Our Commitment to You

  • We take all complaints seriously and will investigate them thoroughly
  • We'll treat you fairly and with respect throughout the process
  • We'll respond promptly and keep you informed
  • We'll learn from complaints to improve our services
  • Making a complaint will not affect your rights or future service from us

2. What Counts as a Complaint?

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction with our service, products, or staff, including (but not limited to):

  • Quality of workmanship or materials
  • Delays or missed appointments
  • Communication issues
  • Billing or payment disputes
  • Damage to your property during installation
  • Behavior or professionalism of our staff
  • Any other aspect of our service that didn't meet your expectations

3. How to Make a Complaint

We want to make it as easy as possible for you to raise concerns. You can contact us in any of the following ways:

By Email (Preferred)

Send your complaint to: contact@sturij.com

Please include "COMPLAINT" in the subject line to ensure it's prioritized.

By Phone

Call us and ask to speak with Mark Walton (Complaints Officer). If he's not immediately available, we'll arrange a convenient time for him to call you back.

In Writing

Post your complaint to:

Mark Walton, Complaints Officer

Storage Innovation Limited

7 Woodland Close

Wetherby, LS22 6BJ

Via Our Website

Log into your customer portal at www.sturij.com/login and submit a complaint through the support request system.

What to Include in Your Complaint:

  • Your name and contact details
  • Your project or order reference (if applicable)
  • A clear description of the issue
  • When the issue occurred
  • Any relevant photos or documentation
  • What outcome you're hoping for (e.g., repair, refund, explanation)

4. Our Complaints Procedure

We follow a clear, step-by-step process to ensure your complaint is handled fairly:

Step 1: Acknowledgment (Within 2 Working Days)

When we receive your complaint, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt (by email or phone)
  • Assign a unique reference number
  • Confirm the name of the person handling your complaint (Mark Walton, Complaints Officer)
  • Provide an expected timeframe for resolution

Step 2: Investigation (Within 5 Working Days)

We will:

  • Gather all relevant information and documentation
  • Speak with any staff involved
  • Contact you if we need more information or clarification
  • Assess the complaint objectively and fairly
  • If needed, arrange to visit your property to inspect the issue

Step 3: Resolution (Within 10 Working Days)

We will provide you with a full written response that includes:

  • Our findings from the investigation
  • Whether we uphold your complaint (fully, partially, or not at all)
  • A clear explanation of our decision
  • What action we'll take to resolve the issue (if applicable)
  • A timeframe for completing any remedial work
  • Information about your right to escalate if you're not satisfied

Note: For complex complaints requiring extensive investigation, we may need additional time. If this is the case, we'll contact you within 10 working days to explain the delay and provide a new timeframe.

5. Possible Outcomes

Depending on the nature of your complaint, we may offer one or more of the following resolutions:

  • A sincere apology and explanation
  • Repair or rectification work at no cost to you
  • Replacement of defective materials or furniture
  • A partial or full refund
  • A goodwill gesture or discount on future services
  • Changes to our processes to prevent recurrence
  • An explanation if we believe the complaint is not upheld, with clear reasoning

6. If You're Not Satisfied With Our Response

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your complaint or the resolution offered, you have the following options:

Escalation Within Sturij

Contact Mark Walton directly at contact@sturij.com to request a review of the complaint and our response. We'll conduct a fresh review and respond within 5 working days.

Alternative Dispute Resolution

You may wish to use an independent mediation or alternative dispute resolution service. We're willing to participate in such processes if it helps reach a fair resolution.

We can recommend services if needed, or you can find accredited providers through:

  • The Civil Mediation Council: www.civilmediation.org
  • Citizens Advice Consumer Service: www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer

Legal Action

You always retain the right to pursue legal action through the courts if you believe you have a valid claim. We recommend seeking independent legal advice before taking this step.

7. Learning From Complaints

We view complaints as valuable opportunities to improve our service. We will:

  • Keep a record of all complaints and their outcomes
  • Analyze patterns and trends to identify areas for improvement
  • Share lessons learned with our team
  • Implement changes to prevent similar issues in the future

8. Confidentiality

All complaints are treated confidentially. Information will only be shared with those who need to know in order to investigate and resolve your complaint. We handle complaint data in accordance with our Privacy & Data Protection Policy.

9. Unreasonable or Vexatious Complaints

While we're committed to addressing all genuine complaints, we reserve the right to decline to investigate complaints that are:

  • Clearly vexatious or made in bad faith
  • Repetitive without new evidence or information
  • Abusive or harassing toward our staff
  • Already fully investigated and resolved

We'll always explain our reasoning if we decide not to investigate a complaint.

10. Policy Review

We review this policy annually to ensure it remains effective and fair. If you have suggestions for how we could improve our complaints process, we'd love to hear from you.
