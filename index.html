The hardware

Engineered to disappear.

Every door, drawer and lift-up in a Sturij room runs on Blum — the Austrian motion hardware the trade builds on. Soft-close BLUMOTION catches the last few degrees and draws it shut without a sound. Tool-free CLIP-on mounting. Fine three-way adjustment. It's the part you never see that decides whether a room still feels new in twenty years.
